## Welcome to Open Heightz

Open Heightz is a community-driven technology initiative dedicated to making practical software, digital education and open-source innovation accessible to everyone.

We believe technological opportunity should not be determined by income, location, educational background or professional experience.

**Our mission:** Build what matters. Share what empowers. Improve what exists.

## Our Projects

| Project | Purpose |

| [DevPath Open](https://github.com/ItsYuhBoyClesh/devpath-open) | Free programming lessons and interactive quizzes |

## Who We Serve

- **Individuals:** Accessible tools and opportunities to build digital skills.
- **Students:** Practical learning resources and real-world development experience.
- **Educational institutions:** Open educational material and collaborative learning opportunities.
- **Developers:** Open-source projects, technical collaboration and learning resources.
- **Startups and businesses:** Practical technology foundations and opportunities for innovation.
- **Nonprofits and communities:** Accessible solutions for everyday technological challenges.

## Our Core Principles

**Accessibility:** Technology should reach people regardless of their circumstances.

**Innovation:** Solve meaningful problems through thoughtful engineering.

**Education:** Knowledge should empower people to create and improve.

**Collaboration:** Great solutions grow through contributions from different people.

**Integrity:** Respect intellectual property, acknowledge contributors and develop responsibly.

## Join Our Community

Everyone is welcome to explore our public projects, report issues, propose improvements, contribute documentation and collaborate through pull requests.

Beginners and experienced developers are equally welcome.

No invitation is required to contribute to public repositories.
